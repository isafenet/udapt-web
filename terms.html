Udapt reads data from Apple Health (or a morning check-in) and produces a daily training and nutrition plan on your device. It is provided as a consumer wellness app for iPhone, with an optional companion Apple Watch app.

Talk to a suitably qualified professional before making significant changes to your training, diet, or if you have an existing injury or health condition. Udapt is not intended for anyone experiencing a medical emergency.
