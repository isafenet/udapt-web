Run, ride, swim. Lift anywhere.

Runs, rides and swims planned around how you've recovered, strength training wherever you are today, and a new Apple Watch app. Udapt 1.3 brings it all into the one plan that adapts each morning.

Running, cycling and swimming

Cardio that fits your training.

Pick your days and your level, and Udapt plans the rest around your strength sessions and how you've recovered.

  • Running: a nine-week run/walk plan to a 30-minute run, or running towards a goal or a race, from 5K to marathon.
  • Cycling and swimming: easy rides, and pool or open-water swims, with plans for beginners through to experienced.
  • Spoken cues and a timer on iPhone, or the whole session on your Apple Watch, saved to Apple Health.
  • Runs, rides and swims from other apps and devices that save to Apple Health count too.

Train anywhere

The gym, home, a hotel, or just the floor.

Choose where you're training for each session, and today's workout changes to suit what's there.

  • Home: tell Udapt what you have, from a pair of dumbbells to a multi-gym.
  • Resistance bands and hotel gyms get their own versions of your plan, so a trip doesn't stop your week.
  • Levels from beginner to experienced, with simpler versions of the big lifts to start, and Udapt tells you when you're ready to move up.

Apple Watch

A new app on your wrist.

  • Your morning check-in, readiness and today's plan.
  • Heart rate as you lift and rest, last time's weights, how-tos and swaps.
  • Every interval of a run, ride or swim, with lengths and pace in the pool.

Progress

See your weeks add up.

  • Weeks in a row with your training days done.
  • This week's sets for each muscle, and your personal records.
  • Share a picture of a finished workout.
